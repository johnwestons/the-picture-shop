THE PICTURE SHOP - PC AND ANDROID 105 PLAYTEST UPDATE

Windows: 0.1.0-test.105
Android: 0.1.0-android.105 (version code 105)
Shared multiplayer protocol: 74

Install the matching build on every multiplayer participant. Builds using an
older protocol, including the previous 104 release, cannot join this version.
The Windows source workspace has the same version and gameplay changes.

WHAT CHANGED

- Courier Prime typewriter UI, clearer balances and notifications, larger mobile
  office pages, scrollable job tickets, and paged Home Guide instructions.
- Multiple completed jobs can share a delivery truck. Player and employee
  deliveries follow the appropriate town/city stops for their loaded pallets.
- Customer pickup trucks travel visibly through town. Traffic recovery can
  clear prolonged stalls without completing or paying for undelivered cargo.
- Revised roads and bridge lanes, sharper GPS guidance, park/campground details,
  and the loading-dock exterior connect the warehouse with the town.
- Saved notices and tutorial guidance, office browser navigation, and an archive
  of receipts and delivery signatures make information easier to find.
- Customer calls, replies, freight and services follow business hours. Existing
  dock work and production can finish after closing. Home delivery charges use
  a 15% fee with a $2 minimum, shown at checkout.
- Existing iOS touch-control support remains in the shared source.

VALIDATION AND DOWNLOADS

The release includes separate Windows and Android installers, SHA256SUMS.txt,
and release-verification.json. The verification file records the exact shared
source commit, package hashes, protocol, test results, signing comparison, and
whether a physical phone was tested. Automated mobile layouts and simulated
multiplayer do not certify physical-device or public-Internet behavior.

This is the project's development playtest release. The Android APK uses the
project's development signing identity; the Windows installer is unsigned.
Download the APK on Android and open it to install/update. Start a fresh test
shop to exercise the new town generation; existing towns keep their geometry.
