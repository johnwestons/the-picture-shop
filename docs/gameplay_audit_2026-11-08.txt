THE PICTURE SHOP - GAMEPLAY FUNCTIONALITY AUDIT
Started: November 8, 2026 (America/New_York)

Scope
Review active gameplay against the repository's gameplay notes, current feature
contracts and recent delivery/grocery instructions. Exercise entry, intermediate
steps, completion, payment/rewards, cancellation, duplicate input, interruption,
save/reload and guest authority. Existing work on delivery, estimates and cutter
maintenance was retained. Relevant disposable saves were reset without making
backups; existing backup files were left alone.

Confirmed gameplay defects fixed
1. Grocery cart routes could not reliably leave the entrance or reach shelves
   and checkout. The center display left insufficient clearance, and the route
   planner did not use the directional cart footprint enforced by movement.
   Opened the cross aisle, aligned planning with collision, and routed to a
   reachable interaction position instead of the center of a shelf.
2. Clicking a shelf again could return the cart when its sprite overlapped the
   shelf target. Shelf targets now take priority while pushing the cart. Cart
   interaction elsewhere and the F return action remain available.
3. A five-pallet printing quote with delivery information exceeded the multiplayer
   packet budget, preventing the reception view from reaching the guest. Quote
   rows now use compact wire tuples, retain named fields in the game, and still
   validate sizes, counts and totals. Multiplayer protocol is now 71; participants
   must use matching builds.

Test and audit repairs
- Estimating's emailButtonCenter helper pointed at the old button position, now
  occupied by another input. It now returns the actual button for the active tab.
- The guest job journey expected employee delivery even though its estimate did
  not offer delivery. It now verifies customer-arranged transportation and runs
  the customer's actual pickup through payment, without rewriting driver state.
- The apartment journey waited for automatic shutdown. It now performs the
  owner's explicit shutdown request at the entrance. Its scripted rider follows
  a road lane, uses the warehouse driveway and parking aisle around parked trucks,
  and brakes inside the current stall boundaries. No exterior teleporting or
  removal of traffic is used to complete those journeys.
- Added gameplay_journey_audit_test.lua to the full suite and a focused
  gameplay-recovery run. Every ordered pair of grocery destinations is exercised
  in solo and shared movement, including repeated selection, changing destination,
  menu pause, checkout access and cart return. Added failed-funds/duplicate-buy,
  cargo save/reload and duplicate-unload checks, plus complete solo air hockey,
  finished-match scoring, opponent disconnect, restart and leave checks.
- Added guest-job-journey focus. Added individual network view diagnostics and
  short/inconsistent compact quote tuple rejection checks.

Verification limits
Most coverage uses real LOVE game modules, controls and simulation driven by
automated fixtures. Physical travel tests drive actual movement/vehicle physics;
some other workflow tests place actors or establish inventory to reach the
mechanic under test. Manual UI checks were limited to launch, menus and saves.
Forced mobile layout is not a physical Android test. This pass does not certify
public Internet multiplayer, separate physical devices, or a newly packaged
installer. No release/APK was built or published. Retired machines excluded from
the active catalog were not reintroduced to satisfy obsolete test expectations.
