Picture Shop strict yard protocol — 2026-11-15
============================================

Behavior
--------
Freight and customer pickup trucks switch from general road driving to a
prescribed line when they enter the shop's receiving area. The controller uses
the existing entry, queue, dock setup, dock, and exit geometry. Road AI cannot
choose another line or overtake while the yard controller owns the truck.

The dock reservation follows a truck through approach, backing, service, and
departure. Waiting trucks advance in dispatch order. Receipt/signature completion
does not release the reservation before the departing exterior vehicle exists.
The exit line retains control through the frontage-road turn until the truck is
aligned in its road lane. A returning company truck follows its own prescribed
route around the loading dock to its home stall.

Receiving trucks stop for 0.75 simulated seconds at the queue head and reversing
setup, reverse straight into the dock, and stop again at the end of the exit
line before returning to road AI. Normal yard speeds are limited to 2.4 m/s
forward and 1.2 m/s reverse. Company forward maneuvers are limited to 2.2 m/s.

Every move sweeps the full truck footprint, including rotation, against static
and dynamic obstacles. A blockage stops movement immediately. After 3 simulated
seconds without progress, the receiving-yard watchdog reports the stall. At
8 seconds it attempts a teleport to the current stage's reserved checkpoint.
Company trucks also attempt checkpoint recovery after 8 seconds. Recovery only
uses a clear destination, retries occupied destinations, respects the dock/exit
reservation, and is available through the existing watchdog Unstick action.

Checkpoint recovery does not sign receipts, load/unload pallets, complete jobs,
award money, or count teleport distance as mileage/fuel usage. Normal movement
still accounts for mileage, fuel, and vehicle service wear. Protocol stage,
reservation target, progress, and stall time survive serialization. Old pickups
already being loaded can restore at the dock only after obtaining its reservation
and finding its complete footprint clear. Older pending departures also restore
one at a time, never over an occupied dock.

Implementation
--------------
src/commute/yard_rail.lua: fixed-line motion, swept collision, safe placement.
src/commute/warehouse_yard.lua: receiving-yard state machine and recovery.
src/commute/warehouse_queue.lua: exclusive dock/exit ownership and queue order.
src/commute/delivery_traffic.lua: road/yard handoff, spawn and migration guards,
  rendering of physical poses, watchdog integration, and shared collision index.
src/commute/company_truck_motion.lua and src/company_delivery_driver.lua:
  employee truck paths, saved motion, exception recovery, and driver handoffs.
src/save_schema/paper.lua: saved protocol and migration validation.

This replaces the company follow-up's general forward AI/steered reverse motion
described in code_audit_2026-11-14.txt with prescribed yard motion. That document's
11,964-check full pass describes the preceding version, not this protocol change.
