WHAT TO TEST

- Choose STORY and follow the illustrated departure from Millhaven, the
  motorcycle arrival at Grandpa's farm, his conversation, and free travel after
  he hands over the shop keys.
- Inspect the closed shop's restoration stages, floor dirt and water, breaker
  and utility actions, and the tutorial notices leading toward grand opening.
- Check the redesigned ride HUD and GPS at normal and narrow screen widths.
  The bike condition card, notices, calendar, clock, map, and mobile controls
  should remain readable and independently usable.
- Review estimates, typed promotion comments, cutter skid selection on touch,
  saved receipts and signatures, and mobile office pages.
- Walk the larger park and campground paths, and check day/night lighting and
  sound in the town.

PLAYTEST SCOPE

This is an in-progress story update for hands-on testing. The host-new-shop
multiplayer lobby, synchronized multiplayer opening, grand-opening cinematic,
and machine power gates remain planned. Start an existing shop or use the current
new-shop flow to test the systems already present.

Automated desktop and mobile core suites passed 11,828 checks each. Every
planned scenario stage had a passing run; scene-exit checks were rerun after
updating their touch path to the shared menu. The populated office measured
about 49 fps in the local performance probe and remains a tuning target.
Automated mobile checks do not substitute for a physical phone test. The Android
APK uses the project's development signing identity; the Windows installer is
unsigned. Test saves are disposable, and a fresh shop is recommended for the
restoration opening and updated town generation.
