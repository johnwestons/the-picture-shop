WHAT'S NEW

- Vehicle steering now supports the smooth angles between the truck sprites.
  Small corrections settle more cleanly, especially in third-person driving.
- Restore the warehouse by reconnecting utilities, fixing the breaker, cleaning
  its generated dirt and oily-water floor patches, repairing equipment, and
  completing the grand opening. Wet and dry footprints appear and fade as you
  walk through the floor.
- Grandpa gives you an additional $420 at the start of the story, on top of the
  normal $180. He gives money, not service kits.
- The restored shop now has a skippable grand-opening sequence: the curtains
  open, the shop banner drops, the door sign flips to OPEN, and business unlocks
  when the final OPEN FOR BUSINESS card appears.
- Continue from Grandpa's handoff into free travel, and visit the shop or your
  apartment in either order.
- Truck deliveries now preserve vehicle ownership, continuous road heading,
  route progress, and multiplayer snapshots across saves and reconnects.
