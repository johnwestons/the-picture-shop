Original finding — fixed in the company-driving follow-up
---------------------------------
P1 (resolved): Company truck departure bypassed collision-aware driving.

At the audited baseline, src/company_delivery_driver.lua:159 (updateExterior) advanced departure from
elapsed wall-clock time. The actors function at line 217 samples a scheduled
route for presentation. Neither applies the live truck movement/collision
controller during that journey. The return branch also places the vehicle
directly in its home stall, without checking that the stall is unoccupied.

A native LÖVE probe installed an obstruction callback that blocks every truck
position. Truck.blocked correctly returned true. The company departure actor
still sampled a speed of 2.8, and updateExterior completed the departure with
zero obstacle queries. This is a confirmed discrepancy, not a road-generation
hypothesis. Evidence: output/code-audit-20261114/departure-probe.txt and the
adjacent departure-probe/main.lua fixture.

Impact: a company vehicle can move through a player, parked vehicle, or blocked
exit, while an incoming freight truck correctly stops for the same obstruction.
Arrival/docking tests for freight do not cover this company-vehicle shortcut.

Follow-up implemented: company departure and return now use physical truck
controls and the shared obstacle/traffic system. Pose and route progress persist
through saves. The driver remains away until parking and the walk back inside
finish. The return route bypasses an occupied freight dock. See the follow-up
validation below; the original probe is retained as pre-fix evidence.

Confirmed issues fixed in this audit
-----------------------------------
1. Dispatch could interrupt shipping or receipt work without a machine assignment.
   The driver selector only considered a subset of employee tasks. A regression
   reproduced an employee being dispatched with _shippingActive set. The selector
   now waits for shipping, inbound unloading, carried cargo, and receipt tasks,
   and excludes employees who are stopping or already away. Machine assignments
   and training continue to finish through the existing reservation mechanism.
   Files: src/company_delivery_driver.lua; src/employee_ai.lua.

2. Employees could leave an operated pallet jack behind when dispatched.
   Dispatch now waits for an empty jack to be parked/released; pending drivers
   run the normal parking action before boarding. Loaded jacks remain with the
   employee's current cargo task. The low-fuel refuel path also rejects an
   employee still operating a jack. Tests exercise reservation, release, and
   subsequent dispatch.

3. Dispatch relabeled employees in other rooms as being in the warehouse.
   Their coordinates were unchanged, so they could begin the exit route at a
   wrong or blocked warehouse position. Dispatch now preserves the room and
   invokes the existing room-travel handoff before walking to the outside door.
   Regression checks preserve the starting pose and require the travel callback.

4. Remote office wage controls assumed every guest was player 2.
   The isolated remote screen had no Runtime.multiplayer object and silently
   fell back to ID 2. A reconnected guest with ID 3 could not propose their own
   wage. The application now passes the current session ID into the screen.
   A regression failed before the change and passed afterward through the actual
   remote office click path.
   Files: src/runtime/multiplayer_events.lua;
          src/screens/workshop_remote/lifecycle.lua; src/tests/guest_payroll_test.lua.

5. Estimated stock-arrival text ignored the 24-hour clock setting.
   JobService also referenced a nonexistent private Runtime.App. It now accepts
   the time-format preference explicitly from the computer screen. The new
   24-hour formatting check passes.
   Files: src/job_service/repeat_jobs.lua; src/screens/computer/email.lua.

6. Coverage gap around the previously fixed email composer crash.
   Existing promotion checks typed and sent text without drawing the independent
   computer screen. Added an actual composer draw to that scenario, covering the
   path which previously crashed on Runtime.Viewport. It passes in native LÖVE.
   File: src/tests/input_status_test.lua.

Cross-system pattern to watch
----------------------------
Several modules name their private context Runtime. A screen's Runtime is not
the application's Runtime: it does not automatically contain App, Viewport, or
multiplayer. The composer crash, guest wage bug and ignored clock preference
all crossed that boundary incorrectly. Future changes should pass the needed
value/service explicitly and exercise an independently-created screen through
both input and drawing. Likewise, dispatch tests need to start from a worker's
current task and room, not only from an idle warehouse worker.

Company-driving follow-up — 2026-11-14
------------------------------------
Replaced the wall-clock/sampled company truck shortcut with a saved physical
vehicle and yard route. Forward travel uses the common road driver; reversing
uses the truck steering/braking physics with a full-footprint obstacle check.
The network's simulation substeps now advance the truck and expose its actual
pose to traffic, player collision, and rendering. Rendering and elapsed wall
clock alone cannot advance a delivery past an obstruction.

Both the box and kei trucks reverse out of their home stalls, leave through the
shop exit, wait for a clear entry on return, drive around the outside of an
occupied freight dock, and park in their own stalls. A truck left at the loading
dock can also depart. The driver remains assigned until parking and the walk
back into the shop finish. Compressed travel between customer stops is retained.

The hidden parked copy stays out of collision and rendering while the employee
is driving. Fuel and service records stay synchronized across interior changes
and saves. Returning the truck resets the player-distance baseline so later
player driving cannot count the employee's yard mileage twice. Boarding starts
a healthy truck's engine; final parking switches it off.

Files: src/commute/company_truck_motion.lua; src/company_delivery_driver.lua;
