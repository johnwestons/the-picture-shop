Story-mode update and GitHub preparation — 2026-11-15

Requested addition
------------------
Critter Credit now finances all four commercial truck listings (new/used box
and kei trucks) and the warehouse forklift. The vehicle dealer and forklift
purchase page have Finance shortcuts; the Credit page has a paginated Fleet tab.
Players review the down payment, principal, APR, term, and monthly payment before
signing. Ownership and the correct new/used vehicle condition are granted through
the existing purchase routines. Only the down payment leaves shop cash.

The new loans share the existing machine credit profile, three-open-account
limit, monthly schedule, late fees, credit reporting, repayment, and lien-release
rules. The calendar labels vehicle/forklift installments as equipment payments.
Old machine loans remain valid. Financed purchase receipts retain the full price,
cash paid, and loan ID. Multiplayer clients send only a listing and request ID;
the host sets prices/terms and commits the purchase. Duplicate requests cannot
create another loan or charge.

The previous mop/spill test failure was an outdated fixture: it sampled the dry
edge of one puddle where the relocated second puddle is visible. The test now
isolates the puddle under test without changing the game's spill placements.
