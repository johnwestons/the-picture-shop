# Chandler & Price platen sample jobs

This station uses the eight-angle Chandler & Price artwork and offers exactly two
starter layouts: a two-pocket folder and a folding carton. It is a local sample
run; the existing office quote, skid, and delivery pipeline is unchanged.

The gameplay follows die-cutting/creasing work rather than ink printing. The
operator locks a die in the chase, sets the sheet gauge and platen packing,
checks grippers, engages impression, and pulls a proof. The real press is
hand-fed; the game now assists feeding one sheet each time the platen opens
after the player starts the run. Cut and scored blanks are collected, inspected, and boxed
flat. A delivery backlog stops the press and requires a fresh proof after
clearing. The throw-off setting is represented by the impression toggle.

The folder reference shows an approximately 18 by 16 inch blank. That exceeds
the C&P 12 × 18 chase documented in the original manufacturer's catalog, so
the starter job uses a scaled 10 × 9 inch version of the same two-panel,
two-pocket layout. The box ticket is a compact carton net with front/back
panels, a glue tab, and top/bottom flaps. Neither station represents final
gluing or assembly: its output is a flat die-cut, scored blank.

References used for the operating model:

- [Chandler & Price Printing Machinery and Equipment, New Series press specifications](https://drukwerkindemarge.org/download/documentatie/Chandler_%26_Price_Printing_Machinery_and_Equipment.pdf): original C&P chase, platen, gripper, throw-off, feed table and speed specifications.
- [Printing United Alliance, Die Cutter Operator](https://www.printing.org/content/2019/03/30/die-cutter-operator): die lock-up, gauges/grippers, make-ready, proof approval, running, and sample inspection sequence.
- [Letterpress Commons, Treadle-operated / Motorized Platen Instruction](https://letterpresscommons.org/treadle-operated-motorized-platen-instruction/): hand-fed open-platen dwell and throw-off use.
- [Letterpress Tutorial, Full-Size Platen Press](https://www.hs-augsburg.de/homes/brownfox/brownfox_dokumente/Handsatzwerkstatt/Letterpress_Tutorial.pdf): C&P component diagram and the die-cutting/scoring use of full-size platen presses.
